Privacy and analytics preferences

Last updated: October 30, 2026.

TYKAIRO-AI publishes information about its software and provides a free demonstration of SuperStore Studio 5. We use optional website analytics only after a visitor chooses to allow them.

Google Analytics 4

After your consent, Google Analytics 4 may use cookies and similar identifiers to measure page views, general device categories, approximate location and interactions such as opening a product page or clicking the SuperStore demo link. We use this information to understand and improve our website. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, private project details or uploaded product content as analytics events.

Analytics is off by default. If you decline, the site continues working. Consent is stored in your browser's local storage. You can change your choice below. The company website and SuperStore demo use different domains, so their consent choices are separate.

SuperStore free demo

SuperStore Studio 5 currently operates on a free, temporary hosting environment. Guest drafts may be lost after a restart or redeployment. Do not enter real customer data, confidential business information or personal details in this experimental demo. Store ZIP export and online payment are not active yet.

Service providers

The public marketing website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The original SuperStore demo runs on Render. If you opt in, Google processes analytics data according to its own policies. Learn about Google privacy at Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choice

Use this button to review or change your consent for analytics on the TYKAIRO-AI website. To change your choice on the separate SuperStore demo domain, open the demo and use its analytics preference notice.

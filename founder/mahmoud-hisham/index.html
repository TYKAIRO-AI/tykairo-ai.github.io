Professional Biography

Mahmoud Hisham is an independent technology founder and AI engineer focused on building artificial intelligence products, intelligent agents, and practical software systems.

His journey in technology began through independent learning, programming, experimentation, and building software projects.

As the founder of TYKAIRO AI, Mahmoud is working toward a long-term vision of creating a global technology company that develops useful AI-powered products, software solutions, and intelligent automation systems.

His areas of focus include AI engineering, AI agents, software development, Python-based tools, workflow automation, and connected technology systems.

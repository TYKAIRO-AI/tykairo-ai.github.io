TYKAIRO AI / ADPILOT

Privacy Policy

Draft for review · Prepared 22 November 2026 · Not yet effective

Draft — do not treat this page as the published policy. This notice must be checked against the deployed product, Meta configuration, actual data-retention settings and the legal identity/contact details of the operator before publication.

This draft explains how TYKAIRO AI ("TYKAIRO", "we", "us") handles information in connection with ADPILOT, an AI-assisted marketing operations application offered primarily as self-hosted software. It distinguishes information processed inside a customer's own ADPILOT installation from the limited information handled by TYKAIRO CONNECT for account authorization.

1. Who operates ADPILOT

ADPILOT is developed and provided by TYKAIRO AI. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to tykairoai@gmail.com. The legal entity name and registered business address must be added here before this policy is published.

2. The self-hosted model

ADPILOT is designed to run in an installation controlled by the customer. Business records created in that installation — such as campaign plans, creative drafts, leads, conversations, workspace records and reports — are stored in the customer's configured application/database environment. TYKAIRO does not receive a central copy of those records merely because the customer installs or uses ADPILOT.

The customer or organization operating the installation is responsible for its hosting environment, administrator access, backups, user permissions and the privacy notices or lawful permissions required for the data it chooses to process.

3. Information TYKAIRO may receive

  • Support and contact information: information you send us, such as your name, email address, installation details and the contents of a support request.
  • Limited service and security information: information needed to operate, protect, troubleshoot and maintain TYKAIRO services, such as service status, software version, request timestamps and security/error diagnostics, where these are enabled. Diagnostic data should not contain passwords, access tokens, AI keys, message contents or lead details.
  • OAuth connection metadata: when an administrator connects Google or Meta through TYKAIRO CONNECT, the service processes the authorization response and stores the provider, workspace identifier, granted permission names, connection status and token expiry/connection timestamps.

4. Google and Meta authorization tokens

TYKAIRO CONNECT currently stores Google and Meta OAuth access tokens, and Google refresh tokens when supplied, in Cloudflare D1 after encrypting them using AES-GCM (256-bit key). The current implementation does not return token values through its ordinary connection-status API. These records are not the same as the customer's campaign, lead or conversation database.

Because the encrypted OAuth records are held in infrastructure operated for TYKAIRO CONNECT, this draft does not claim that TYKAIRO has zero technical or administrative access to that infrastructure. Access to production infrastructure must be restricted to authorized operators and protected with appropriate access controls. The deployed access-control and key-management arrangements must be verified before publication.

Disconnecting a provider through the supported connection endpoint deletes that provider's connection record for the installation. A separate request can be sent to the contact address above. Any backup copies, provider-side records and legally required records may be subject to separate retention periods that must be documented before this policy becomes effective.

5. Information accessed from connected platforms

When a customer authorizes Meta or Google, ADPILOT/CONNECT can request only the permissions configured for the integration and actually granted by the account holder. Depending on the enabled features, this may include ad-account, campaign, Page or Google service information. The exact data available depends on the permissions, selected assets, provider terms and features enabled by the customer.

Meta and Google process information under their own privacy policies and platform terms. Customers should review those terms and ensure they have authority to connect the relevant business assets.

6. AI providers and external services

ADPILOT can be configured to use an AI provider selected by the customer, including an OpenAI-compatible provider, Ollama or OmniRoute. When a user invokes an AI feature, the relevant prompt and information selected or required for that feature may be sent to the configured provider. The provider may process that information under its own terms and privacy policy. Customers should not submit personal or confidential information to an AI provider unless they are authorized to do so and have configured an appropriate provider and data-processing arrangement.

Where a customer uses a local AI provider, processing may remain within the customer's environment, subject to that customer's network and deployment configuration.

7. Purposes and legal grounds

Information is used to provide and maintain the software and integrations, authenticate connections, protect services, respond to support requests, diagnose faults, meet applicable legal obligations and improve reliability. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, TYKAIRO will identify the appropriate basis for each processing activity (such as contract, legitimate interests, consent or legal obligation) in the final published notice.

8. Sharing and service providers

TYKAIRO does not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by infrastructure and integration providers needed to operate the service, including Cloudflare for TYKAIRO CONNECT and Meta or Google when a user chooses to connect those platforms. AI providers are selected/configured by the customer for AI features. A final provider list, locations, contractual safeguards and applicable transfer arrangements must be confirmed before publication.

9. Security

We intend to apply data minimization, least-privilege access, encryption for OAuth tokens at rest, secure transport, access controls and secret-redaction practices. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. The actual production controls, key rotation/recovery process, audit logging and incident-response procedure must be tested and documented before this notice is published.

10. Retention and deletion

Customer application data is retained in the customer's self-hosted environment according to that customer's configuration and deletion practices. TYKAIRO CONNECT retains OAuth connection records while the connection is active and until it is disconnected or a valid deletion request is processed, subject to any documented backup or legal retention requirements. Exact backup and log retention periods must be added before publication.

To request deletion of information held by TYKAIRO, email tykairoai@gmail.com from the account email where possible and include the product name and installation/workspace identifier. Do not email passwords, access tokens or secret keys. For customer-hosted application records, contact the administrator of that ADPILOT installation.

For Meta-related deletion instructions, see ADPILOT Data Deletion Instructions.

11. Children's privacy

ADPILOT is a business software product and is not intended for children. Do not use it to knowingly collect children's personal information unless the customer has an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards.

12. International processing

Self-hosted customer data may be stored in the location selected by the customer. TYKAIRO CONNECT and external providers may process limited information in other countries. The final policy must identify relevant processing locations and safeguards based on the production configuration.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice as the product and its data practices change. The published version will show its effective date and material changes.

14. Contact

Privacy requests and questions: tykairoai@gmail.com.

البيانات التي قد تصل إلى TYKAIRO

قد نتلقى بيانات التواصل التي يرسلها العميل للدعم، ومعلومات تشغيل وأمان محدودة عند تفعيلها، وبيانات وصفية لازمة لإدارة اتصال Google أو Meta، مثل معرّف مساحة العمل ونوع المزود والصلاحيات الممنوحة وحالة الاتصال وتواريخ انتهاء الرمز.

رموز ربط Meta وGoogle

وفق التنفيذ الحالي، تحفظ TYKAIRO CONNECT رموز OAuth الخاصة بـ Meta وGoogle، ورموز التحديث الخاصة بـ Google عند توفرها، داخل Cloudflare D1 بعد تشفيرها باستخدام AES-GCM بمفتاح 256 بت. واجهة حالة الاتصال العادية لا تعرض قيم هذه الرموز. لكن لأنها محفوظة في بنية تحتية تديرها الخدمة، لا يصح أن نزعم أن TYKAIRO لا تملك أي إمكانية تقنية أو إدارية للوصول إلى هذه البنية. يجب تقييد الوصول والتحقق من ضوابط الإنتاج قبل نشر السياسة.

مزودو الذكاء الاصطناعي

قد تُرسل المعلومات التي يختارها المستخدم أو يحتاجها طلب الميزة إلى مزود الذكاء الاصطناعي الذي ضبطه العميل. ويخضع هذا المزود لشروطه وسياسة الخصوصية الخاصة به. ويمكن أن تتم المعالجة محليًا عند استخدام مزود محلي، بحسب إعدادات العميل.

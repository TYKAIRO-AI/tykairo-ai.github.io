Data Deletion Instructions

Draft for review · Prepared 22 November 2026 · Not yet effective

Draft — do not submit this URL to Meta or publish it until the deletion workflow and contact process have been tested.

ADPILOT is designed as self-hosted software. The steps below distinguish data stored in a customer's own installation from OAuth connection records handled by TYKAIRO CONNECT.

1. Delete data from a customer-hosted ADPILOT installation

  1. Contact the administrator who operates the ADPILOT installation.
  2. Ask the administrator to delete the relevant workspace, user account or records using the installation's supported deletion and database-management procedures.
  3. Ask the administrator to remove any customer-controlled backups containing the data, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

TYKAIRO cannot directly delete records from a customer's private server unless the customer separately grants access for support.

2. Disconnect Meta or Google

In ADPILOT, use the integration's disconnect action. The current TYKAIRO CONNECT API deletes the corresponding stored connection record when the supported disconnect endpoint is called. You may also revoke the app's access from the relevant Meta or Google account settings.

3. Request deletion of information held by TYKAIRO

Email tykairoai@gmail.com with the subject “ADPILOT Data Deletion Request”. Include:

  • The email address associated with the request;
  • The ADPILOT installation or workspace identifier (do not include access tokens);
  • Whether the request concerns Meta, Google, support correspondence or another TYKAIRO-held record.

We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request and prevent unauthorized deletion. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, AI provider keys or other secrets by email.

4. Processing and confirmation

After verifying a request, TYKAIRO will identify the relevant records it controls, delete eligible records and confirm completion or explain any legal/technical retention exception. The final published instructions must state a concrete response timeframe and backup-deletion schedule once these have been adopted and tested.

5. Meta platform data requests

For data obtained through Meta, users may also revoke the app's access in their Meta account settings. TYKAIRO will process deletion requests for data it controls in accordance with the final published privacy policy and applicable Meta platform requirements.

Privacy contact: tykairoai@gmail.com.

تعليمات حذف البيانات

هذه مسودة للمراجعة وليست صفحة نهائية. بيانات ADPILOT الموجودة على خادم العميل يحذفها مسؤول التثبيت. أما بيانات ربط Meta وGoogle الموجودة لدى TYKAIRO CONNECT، فيمكن فصل الاتصال من ADPILOT، أو إرسال طلب حذف إلى tykairoai@gmail.com مع ذكر معرّف مساحة العمل ونوع البيانات المطلوبة دون إرسال أي رموز وصول أو كلمات مرور.

بعد التحقق من هوية مقدم الطلب، ستتم مراجعة السجلات التي تتحكم فيها TYKAIRO وحذف ما يجوز حذفه، مع توضيح أي استثناء قانوني أو فني. يجب تحديد مدة الاستجابة وجدول حذف النسخ الاحتياطية قبل نشر الصفحة رسميًا.

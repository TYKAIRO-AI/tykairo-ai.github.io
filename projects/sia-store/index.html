PRODUCT OVERVIEW

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES

Research local business opportunities and manage follow-ups.

A complete look

SIA Store is presented as a business-discovery and prospect-follow-up workspace. It connects location or category-based business research with storing relevant leads and planning the next outreach steps.

The problem it solves

Potential customers are easily lost when business research is separate from outreach notes and follow-up priorities.

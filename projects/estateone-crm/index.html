PRODUCT OVERVIEW

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES

Manage properties, buyers and deal progress with clarity.

A complete look

EstateOne CRM connects real-estate units, enquiries, buyers, sales stages, bookings and follow-up records. It gives agents and small sales teams a practical structure for managing the journey from initial interest to deal follow-through.

The problem it solves

Separating property lists from customer conversations makes it easy to lose the history and current status of an opportunity.

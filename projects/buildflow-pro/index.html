PRODUCT OVERVIEW

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES

Control project progress and construction costs in one place.

A complete look

BuildFlow Pro is a construction-management workspace for project records, client details, jobs, costs, suppliers, workforce and operational progress. It helps teams connect daily site activity to the overall project picture.

The problem it solves

Site updates, receipts and supplier notes are often spread across multiple files and conversations, making cost follow-up and accountability harder.

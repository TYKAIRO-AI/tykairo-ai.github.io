WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES

Centralize your local AI and developer workflows.

A complete look

TYKAIRO Command Center is a dashboard-oriented workspace for navigating technical projects and AI tools from one interface. It keeps related functions and available status information grouped by purpose.

The problem it solves

Multiple local tools are harder to oversee when every workflow has its own disconnected entry point.

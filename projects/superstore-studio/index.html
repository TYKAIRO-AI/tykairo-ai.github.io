PRODUCT OVERVIEW

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES

Organize your online store from products to presentation.

A complete look

SuperStore Studio is a storefront-management workspace for organizing product information and the way a shop appears to visitors. It brings catalog preparation and store presentation into a focused interface.

The problem it solves

An online shop needs a consistent product catalog and clear presentation. Managing everything in disconnected places increases errors.

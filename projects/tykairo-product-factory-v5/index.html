PRODUCT OVERVIEW

WHAT IT DOES

A unified workbench for managing AI product ideas, files, skill editing, quality-review steps, visual assets and publishing workflows.

How it helps

A unified workbench for managing AI product ideas, files, skill editing, quality-review steps, visual assets and publishing workflows.

The problem it solves

Creating a digital product often requires switching between prompts, editors, test reports, image tools and publishing checklists. Product Factory brings these activities into one workflow-oriented dashboard.

CORE FEATURES

BUILT FOR THE WORK.

01

Product dashboard and pipeline

Track product entries, recent activity and where each item sits in the workflow.

02

Skill Editor + AI Chat

Use a dedicated area for product and skill editing with AI-oriented assistance where configured.

03

QA and review workspace

Organize test attempts, review findings and follow-up actions in the QA Lab workflow.

04

Creative assets and publishing

Keep 3D/visual asset work and packaging or publishing stages connected to the product pipeline.
